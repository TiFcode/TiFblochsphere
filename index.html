The Bloch sphere · compact theory

A pure one-qubit state is a point on the surface of a unit sphere. The north pole is |0⟩, the south pole is |1⟩, and every other point represents a superposition with a relative phase.

|ψ⟩ = cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + e^(iφ) sin(θ/2)|1⟩

What the coordinates mean

θ is the polar angle measured down from +Z. φ is the azimuth around Z. The Cartesian Bloch vector is x = sinθ cosφ, y = sinθ sinφ, z = cosθ.

Measurements

Measuring in the computational Z basis gives P(0) = (1 + z)/2 and P(1) = (1 − z)/2. The X and Y axes correspond to different measurement bases, so rotating the sphere changes which question you ask of the qubit.

Rotations and gates

A physical rotation around X, Y, or Z moves the state vector along the sphere. In the lab, use the activity menu, quick state buttons, sliders, or drag directly on the sphere. The current objective is always shown in the mission card.
